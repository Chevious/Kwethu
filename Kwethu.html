Accessible SRH Education & Care for All

TunaCare is a youth-focused platform combining USSD, WhatsApp, and web tools to deliver reliable sexual and reproductive health support across Sub-Saharan Africa.

Download App

Why KwethuSonkhe?

We address youth health stigma, lack of access, and digital inequality by combining tech and community-driven solutions. We use gamification, anonymity, and multilingual content to empower young people and ensure everyone can access care and information.

Core Features

🧠 Interactive SRH Modules

Gamified lessons, quizzes, and comics to teach sexual and reproductive health in a fun, engaging way.

📱 USSD & WhatsApp Access

Designed for low-data users. Use via basic phones and chatbots for instant support and privacy.

🩺 Anonymous Telemedicine

Private access to certified health providers. No judgment, just care when you need it most.

🔐 Access Your rewards

10% of revenue goes into a fund to provide SRH medicines and services to underserved users.

♿ Accessibility First

Inclusive design for visual, hearing, cognitive, and motor impairments. Local languages and audio options available.

🌍 Multilingual & Localized

Supports English, Kinyarwanda, Swahili, and more. Adapts content to cultural context and youth slang.

Inclusive By Design

We co-design with users living with disabilities and follow WCAG standards to ensure everyone can use TunaCare with ease and dignity.
